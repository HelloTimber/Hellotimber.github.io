Beyond Citadel

Drone

In the Grand Army, drones are often used as autonomous mobile turrets...

They are simple weapons designed for cheap kills, but you can never be too careful…

Tracker

These Tracker drones are small, light scout drones...If they find you, they will approach and fire warning shots at you…

Sentry

The "Sentry Drone" is a standard defense drone...It is highly serviceable and reliable and is used in environments where it is difficult to deploy personnel...

Homing

"Homing Drones" are high-end drones capable of firing guided energy projectiles. They are rather high-end products for drones, and they are the only drones with exceptional performance.

Tripod

"Tripods" are heavy base defense drones, often used when tanks cannot be used for various reasons...These types of weapons often meet sad fates. Good thing they can now be unmanned. Don't worry. They're enough of a threat to you in the flesh.
