[Chapter 7 - The End of the World]

[Intermission]

The fate that awaited these fake angels was harsh. Wings were broken, flesh was torn and limbs were lost.
Each time they lost their limbs and organs, they replaced them with cybernetics.

[7-0]

In order to help the last remaining humanity, the Machine Angels chose to network directly with all of them.
It was a necessary choice to heal the loneliness of humanity that had lost 99.99% of their kind.

[7-1]

The Enlightenment Waves released by the Sleeping God contaminated the psyche of humans
through the Network Terminal implants and turned them into puppets without free will.

[7-2]

The destruction of the Sleeping God revealed that the effects of the Enlightenment Wave were permanent.
Its victims have become mindless servants of the Millennium Kingdom.

[7-3]

Living without the Blessing did not protect people from the Enlightenment Wave.
It merely made them less susceptible to it — the wave was still capable of controlling their psyche.

[7-4]

The details of the similarities that exist between the transfer of memories between the units of Divine Object - 919 Martyr
and the transformation/overwriting of personality by the Enlightenment Wave are unclear.

[7-B]

Traces of the time when the Martyr originally existed were thoroughly erased by the Trumpeters of the Apocalypse.

[7-S]

Despite thorough information gathering by the Machine Angels,
its original name, nationality, language, blood relations and all other identities are unknown.
