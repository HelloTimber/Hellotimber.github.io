Beyond Citadel

Auto-Turret

These are automated stationary cannons with slightly higher firepower.

They often appear in defensive roles, but can never take the initiative in combat situations.

Plasma Turret

These "Plasma Turrets" are the most common turrets in the Grand Army. They find you and shoot you. Simple, isn't it?

Floater Turret

These "Floater Turrets" are underwater turrets equipped with multiple flechette dart projectors. Watch out for them. They will often ambush you and shoot you from above or below.

Autocannon

The "Auto-Cannons" are heavy turrets for stronghold defense. They have thick armor and high firepower, but if you don't jump into the lines of fire yourself, you won't be full of holes by them.

Sam Turret

These "SAM Turrets" are guided missile platforms often deployed at high altitudes...The guided missiles they fire have excellent tracking capabilities, but it has been reported that they can be shot down by small arms fire...
