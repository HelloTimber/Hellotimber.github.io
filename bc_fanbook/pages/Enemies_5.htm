Beyond Citadel

Aircraft

Tidal locks and magnetic storms, as well as the proliferation of high-energy laser weapons, have reduced the overwhelming dominance of aircraft as in the Iron Age.

Most aircraft in use today do not engage in air-to-air combat, but rather serve as armored aerial artillery with high firepower.

Quad Flyer

The "Quad Flyer" is a small aircraft designed to accompany and support infantry...It is so-called because it is equipped with a quadruple plasma cannon...

Gunship

The XF-96A "Hummingbird" Gunship is a medium sized aircraft designed for both air-to-ground and air-to-air combat. ..It is equipped with guided missiles and machine guns for both ground and air-to-air combat.

VTOL Attacker

The XA-88B "Juggernaut" VTOL attacker is a heavy aircraft whose main purpose is to sweep the ground. ..It is equipped with numerous firearms and is a threat to ground forces, but its armor is not as thick as its firepower...
