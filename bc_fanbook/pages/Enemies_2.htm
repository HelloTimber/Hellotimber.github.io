Beyond Citadel

Enemy Soldier

Cultist - Acolyte/Adept

The Cultists are the ordinary non-combatant members of the Citadel...After the exposure to the Enlightening wave, they seemed to fight against you with their ritual instruments...Acolytes hit you directly and adepts throw their instruments at you.

Suicider - Suffer/Kamikaze

The Suiciders are ascetics of the citadel who practice a physically painful faith...They are so self-sacrificing and tend to commit desperate attacks... Sufferers toss grenades and kamikazes run at you and attempt attacks.

Gunslinger - Single/Akimbo

The Gunslingers are the patrol unit responsible for maintaining security in the Citadel...They are poorly armed with only pistols and are unlikely to be obstacles to you...Some of them have two pistols, but their threat levels are unlikely to be much different.

Trooper - Scout/Shielder

The Troopers are light infantry whose primary mission is security and patrol duties and are armed with 8-gauge shotguns...Some units are equipped with ballistic shields to take advantage of their light equipment.

Grunt - Rifleman/Grenadier

The Grunts are heavily-armed infantry units who form the core of the Grand Army,..They are armed with obsolete but reliable A1 auto-rifles and hand grenades.

Commando - Infiltrator/Frogman

The Commandos is a scout unit whose main purpose is reconnaissance missions...They are normally valuable units with infiltration tactics, but that will no longer be possible because of the effect of the Enlightening Wave.

Rocketeer

The Rocketeers are special units equipped with AT launchers and thick armors to protect the users from launchers' back blast...However they have no capability to protect the users from direct hits of bullets.

Hunter

The Hunters are sniper units armed with powerful 20 mm whale guns. Their fire might be accurate, but your helmet will trace the shooter's position. Treat them well.

Machinegunner

The machine gunners are special units whose main purpose is defensive combat and are armed with heavy machine guns with shields...Their shields are highly protective, but do not conceal the entire bodies of the shooters, making them extremely vulnerable to accurate sniping and explosions

Howitzer

The Howitzers are special units whose purpose is indirect mortar bombardment...They are formidable opponents but have no way of fighting back against a frontal attack. Once you get close to them, they are easy to neutralize.

Incinerator

The Incinerators are combat engineering units, armoured with heavy cybernetics and diesel guns...Their armours protect them from the heat of the napalm, and they are unlikely to be harmed by small bullets.

Inquisitor

The Inquisitors are heavy cybernetics units deployed in situations that cannot be dealt with by normal patrol and/or infantry units...The plasma rifles attached to their right arms fire barrages of plasma bolts that even you would not be safe from if you were to take a direct hit.

Priest

The Priests are high-ranked members of the Citadel and are not supposed to be appeared in combat situations, usually. If you find them in firefights, their defence lines are collapsing.

Wallgunner

The wallgunners are high-ranked defence units equipped with heavy cybernetics and large-bore wallguns...They are tough and reliable ones who fight in the face of the enemy's bullets at the defence lines. In this case, the enemy is you.
