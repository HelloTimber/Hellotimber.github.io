Beyond Citadel

Landwhale

It is no exaggeration to say that land whales are the dominant species on the planet today...

At the same time, they are a valuable resource. Their hardened bodies, capable of working on the day side, contain fat that can be used as fuel.

Warrior

"Warrior" class land whales are the hatchlings and the smallest of them. ..There are two types of them; ones who run toward you and exhale high-temperature flammable gas at you. ..The other ones who keep away from you and spit strong acid at you.

Tanker

These "Tank" class are medium-sized landwhales. ..They grow missiles and plasma bolts inside their bodies and protect their hives...

Fortress

Only a few hatchlings can grow to this "Fortress" class land whales and it's a rare opportunity to face this threat. They generate homing energy projectiles inside their bio-reactors and overwhelm you with the barrages of them.

Hive

The "Hive" class land whale has no fighting ability of its own, but has the ability to give birth to "warrior class" land whales.
