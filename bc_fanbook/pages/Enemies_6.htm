Beyond Citadel

Walker

Mobile weapons with walking mechanisms, both manned and unmanned, are classified as walkers...

Some are spacious and comfortable to ride in...while others are so small it's hard to imagine how a single human could fit inside.

Battlewalker

The "Battle Walkers", early unmanned walkers, are large for their performance and are often used for rear patrol duties. ..They may look outdated and seem to have trouble getting up and down stairs, ..but they are mobile weapons, so don't underestimate them.

Power Armor

The X-99 Mobile Assault Platform 'Goliath' is a power armor widely used by the Grand Army...It is an excellent and reliable weapon that does not require a neural link to operate...

Marauder

The "Marauder" is the largest walker used by the Grand Army. Its size makes it look more like a walking tank than power armor, and fortunately, you will rarely encounter it in indoor combat.

Tallboy

The "Tallboy" class cybernetic power armors are coffins that the Grand Army has built for their wounded soldiers. They are based on the 'Sentinel' class mobile turret, or a 'coffin' built to preserve the remaining living tissues of the Angel Harkonnen. The only way to get into this thing is to be dismembered and medicated, rejoicing as it is their highest honor, until they lose their original selves.

Iron Maiden

The "Iron Maiden" power armor is the smallest armor designed for confined spaces, ..but its firepower and armor are no less than a Goliath PA. ..In other words, the poor guys facing this thing are going up against a Goliath ..with small arms in a place where they can't bring in big guns.
